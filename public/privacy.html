Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information BricksVault ("BricksVault", "we", "us") collects when you use the BricksVault app and website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using BricksVault you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we process your email address and an authentication identifier through our authentication provider. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the basic profile identifiers those providers return (such as your email). We do not receive your Google or Apple password.
  • Your collection & portfolio. The sets and minifigures you add, quantities, condition, purchase prices, purchase dates, storage locations, personal notes, wishlist items, and showcase selections.
  • Content you submit. Photos you upload, reviews and ratings you write, and catalog corrections or contributions you send for moderation.
  • Preferences & settings. Display name, public handle, currency, notification preferences, an optional Discord webhook URL, an optional Kids-Mode PIN (stored only as a one-way hash), and connected-integration status (e.g. Google Sheets sync, Brickset).
  • Device & technical data. We use privacy-preserving, aggregate analytics and standard server logs (such as request metadata) to keep the service reliable and secure. We store some data locally on your device (for offline use and guest mode) using your browser's storage.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core features: tracking your collection, valuing sets, forecasting, wishlists, and showcases.
  • To sync your data across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To send the notifications you enable (for example, price-drop alerts).
  • To moderate user-submitted content and protect the service from abuse and bots.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the app.

AI photo identification

When you use the AI scan feature to identify a set from a photo, the image may be sent to third-party AI providers (for example, Google or OpenAI/OpenRouter) solely to return an identification. On-device identification, when available on your device, runs locally and does not send the image off your device. We do not use your photos to train our own models.

Third-party services

We rely on the following categories of third parties to run BricksVault. Each processes data only as needed to provide its function:

  • Authentication & database/storage hosting — to manage sign-in and store your data.
  • Cloud hosting & content delivery — to serve the app and store uploaded images.
  • AI providers — for photo identification, as described above.
  • Catalog & pricing sources (such as Rebrickable, BrickLink, eBay, PriceCharting, and Brickset) — we query these for catalog details and market prices; this generally involves set identifiers, not your personal information.
  • Bot protection — to distinguish humans from automated abuse.
  • Optional integrations you enable — such as Google Sheets sync or Brickset, which operate only after you connect them.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. Your collection is private by default. Some information becomes visible to others only when you choose to make it so — for example, publishing a public profile or showcase, or submitting a review or contribution for public display. You can change these choices in the app.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see below), we delete your associated data as described. Some minimal records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction. You can view and edit your profile, collection, and preferences directly in the app.
  • Deletion. You can permanently delete your account and its data from inside the app: open the Me tab and choose Delete account. This erases your vault, wishlist, showcase, uploaded photos, reviews, contributions, and preferences. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Notifications. You can turn notifications on or off in the app or in your device settings.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data in transit and at rest, including encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and continuously improve our safeguards.

Children's privacy

BricksVault is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Kids Mode is an optional, PIN-gated simplified view of an existing account's data; it is not a separate service for children and does not collect additional personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International users

BricksVault may process and store information in countries other than yours. Where required, we take steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through in-app notice.

Contact
