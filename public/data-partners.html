Data partners

Confirmed market data partners for BricksVault.

PriceCharting

PriceCharting is our confirmed market-data partner. Its verified sold-price history helps anchor values where identities are verified, and its yearly sales volume powers the liquidity badge ("sells fast / sells slow").

We link to PriceCharting product pages only through a verified numeric mapping for that exact set. When no verified mapping exists, we link the PriceCharting homepage and label it as the source homepage — never as a product page.

How credit works. PriceCharting is credited on a set only when its verified data actually contributed to that set's valuation. If PriceCharting contributed, the set page shows an attributed credit with a direct link; otherwise nothing is claimed.

Valuation methodology

Values shown across BricksVault are estimates from daily blend snapshots. For technical details on independent signal families, confidence tiers, and formulas, see our pricing methodology.
