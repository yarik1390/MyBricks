Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data BricksVault ("BricksVault", "we", "us") collects when you use the BricksVault app and website at bricksvault.app, why we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have.

Who is responsible

BricksVault is run by an independent developer, who is the controller of your personal data. For anything about this policy or your data, write to support@bricksvault.app.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and an account identifier, handled by our authentication provider. If you sign in with Google, we receive the basic profile details Google returns (such as your email). We never receive your Google password.
  • Your collection. The sets and minifigures you add, quantities, condition, purchase prices and dates, sales, storage locations, notes, wishlist items, sub-collections and showcase choices. If you use the Family vault, the members of your household can see the shared collection.
  • Content you submit. Photos you upload, reviews, catalog corrections and feedback you send.
  • Swaps and chat. If you turn on swaps, other collectors can see your public handle and the sets you offer. We store your swap requests, the notes you add and the chat messages you exchange. Messages are checked automatically for contact details (such as phone numbers or email addresses) so we can warn you before you share them. If you report someone, the report and the reported person's recent messages in that swap are kept so we can review it. Blocks and reports are stored with your account.
  • Photos you scan. When you identify a set from a photo, the photo is sent to an AI provider (see below) to return a result. We do not store scan photos.
  • Preferences. Display name, public handle, currency, language, notification choices, an optional Discord webhook URL, an optional Kids Mode PIN (stored only as a one-way hash) and the status of integrations you connect (such as Google Sheets, Brickset or Rebrickable). Access tokens for integrations are stored encrypted.
  • Approximate country. To pick your shop and currency on first launch, we use the country of your mobile network (Android) or the country suggested by your IP address. We do not use GPS and never ask for location permission.
  • Purchases. If you buy BricksVault Pro in the Android app, Google Play processes the payment and we never see your card details. Our subscription provider RevenueCat tells us the purchase status (product, dates, renewal or cancellation) linked to your account so Pro unlocks on your devices. On the web, supporter purchases go through Patreon, which tells us whether you are an active supporter.
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications in the Android app, Firebase Cloud Messaging issues a device token that we store so we can send the alerts you turned on. It is deleted when you turn notifications off or delete your account.
  • Emails. If you turn on email alerts or the weekly digest, we use your email address to send them.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs (such as IP address, device and request details) to keep the service running and secure, cookieless aggregate analytics, counters that enforce usage limits, and data stored on your device (for offline use and guest mode).

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the service you signed up for (contract): your account, collection tracking, valuations, sync across devices, wishlist alerts, swaps and chat, Pro features and the integrations you connect.
  • To keep BricksVault safe and working (legitimate interest): bot protection, usage limits, abuse and fraud prevention, checking chat messages for contact details, handling reports, fixing bugs and improving the app.
  • Things you choose to turn on (consent): push notifications, email alerts and the weekly digest, a public profile or showcase. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app, without affecting what happened before.
  • Legal obligations: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

AI features

Photo identification, shelf scanning, box grading and the AI advisor send the photo or question (and, for the advisor, a summary of your collection) to an AI provider to produce a result. Providers we use include Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude), Cloudflare Workers AI, Brickognize and models reached through OpenRouter. Some providers keep requests for a limited time for abuse monitoring, and some models offered free through OpenRouter may log requests or use them to improve their service. We do not use your photos to train our own models. If you add your own Gemini or OpenAI key, requests go to that provider under your own account.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only with service providers that process it on our behalf, and only as needed for their task:

  • Supabase: sign-in and account identity.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, database, photo storage, Workers AI, bot protection (Turnstile) and cookieless web analytics.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Play Billing, web fonts, Gemini AI, and Google Sheets if you connect it.
  • RevenueCat: purchase status for Pro in the Android app. Patreon: supporter status on the web.
  • Resend: delivery of alert and account emails.
  • AI providers listed above, for the AI features.
  • Discord, Brickset and Rebrickable: only if you connect them yourself.

Catalog and pricing sources (such as Rebrickable, BrickLink, eBay, PriceCharting and Brickset) receive set identifiers from us, not your personal data. Some set pages link to stores such as Amazon or LEGO.com; some of these are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission. When you follow a link, that store's own privacy policy applies.

Other collectors see your information only where you choose: a public profile or showcase, reviews, your swap offers and the messages you send in a swap chat. Supporters with a public profile may appear on the supporter wall. We may also disclose data if the law requires it or to protect users from harm.

International transfers

Our providers store and process data in several countries, including the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on the provider's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Account, collection, preferences and uploaded photos: while your account exists, and deleted when you delete it.
  • Swap requests and chat messages: while both people in the swap have an account. They are deleted when either person deletes their account.
  • Reports: as long as needed to review them and protect users, then deleted.
  • Scan photos: not stored after the result is returned.
  • Server logs and usage counters: kept for a short period for security and limits.
  • Guest mode data: stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU and UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access and correct your data. Most of it can be viewed and edited directly in the app.
  • Delete your account and data. In the app or on bricksvault.app, open the Me tab and choose Delete account. This immediately and permanently erases your vault, wishlist, showcase, uploaded photos, reviews, swaps and chat, and preferences. You can also ask us by email at support@bricksvault.app, without reinstalling the app.
  • Take your data with you: export your collection as a file from the app.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest, and restrict processing in some cases.
  • Withdraw consent for notifications, emails or a public profile at any time in the app.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, write to support@bricksvault.app. We will reply within one month.

Security

We use encrypted connections, encrypted storage of integration tokens, access controls and rate limits to protect your data. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

Children

You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age for digital consent in your country, such as 15 in France or 16 in Germany) to create an account. BricksVault is not directed to children. Kids Mode is an optional, PIN-protected simplified view of a parent's existing account; it is not a separate account and does not collect extra personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Write to support@bricksvault.app.
