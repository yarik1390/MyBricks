Delete your BricksVault account

Last updated: 7 November 2026

You can permanently delete your BricksVault account and its data at any time, from the app or from the website. You don't need to reinstall the app.

Delete it yourself

  1. Open the BricksVault app, or go to bricksvault.app and sign in.
  2. Open the Me tab.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Or ask us

Email support@bricksvault.app from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We will do it and confirm within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It erases your account, vault, wishlist, showcase, uploaded photos, reviews and contributions, swaps and chat messages, family vault membership, notification tokens and preferences. Reports you sent about other users may be kept for a limited time so we can finish reviewing them. Data stored on your own device in guest mode stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Pro subscription: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).
