Data sources & partners

Where BricksVault's market data comes from — and what each provider actually does.

Confirmed data partner

PriceCharting

PriceCharting is our confirmed market-data partner. Its verified sold-price history helps anchor values where identities are verified, and its yearly sales volume powers the liquidity badge ("sells fast / sells slow").

We link to PriceCharting product pages only through a verified numeric mapping for that exact set. When no verified mapping exists, we link the PriceCharting homepage and label it as the source homepage — never as a product page.

Sources, and the role each plays

Every other provider below is a named source, not a partner. Each plays a different role in the valuation; no single source sets a value alone.

SourceRoleWhat it contributes
BrickLinkSoldGuide values for new and used sets — a primary sold-condition signal.
eBaySold AskingRealized sold comps and, as a weaker fallback, live asking prices.
BrickOwlAskingNew and used marketplace asking prices as a corroborating signal.
BrickEconomyModeledModeled values and growth forecasts for sets with enough history.
BricksetReferenceRatings, review counts, MSRP, launch/exit dates and set facts — quality and catalog data, not prices.
RebrickableReferenceThe base LEGO catalog, images and parts relationships.
PriceChartingSoldVerified sold prices (via verified identity mappings) and yearly sales volume. The one confirmed partner.
How credit works. A source is named on a set only when its verified data actually contributed to that set's valuation. If PriceCharting contributed, the set page shows a "valued with PriceCharting" credit with a direct link; otherwise nothing is claimed. Independent markets are counted honestly — everything that ultimately observes eBay counts once, so PriceCharting and eBay never masquerade as two independent confirmations.

Freshness

Sources are refreshed on their own schedules and carry their own timestamps. Values shown are estimates from daily blend snapshots — what a patient sale might bring, not money in hand. See How we price for the full method: identity checks, independent families, confidence tiers and honest gaps.
